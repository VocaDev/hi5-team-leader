## 1. FLUTURA — the problem, general first, then our example (0:00–1:15)

**General (Slide 1):**
> Good afternoon, we're Hi5.
>
> Think of any business that sells **work done by a team in the field**: events, cleaning, installation, maintenance, construction.
> **Every time a client says "yes", a coordination problem starts.** Several people, vehicles and equipment have to be in the right place, at the right time, without clashing with the jobs already booked.
> Today that lives **in one person's head and on WhatsApp**. And when something changes, **one quick fix often breaks another job.**

**Example (Slide 2):**
> Here's a real case. Magic Events is an events business in Kosovo; the owners gave us permission to share this.
> In one difficult case, coordinating a team took **about four hours**. Once, a worker was moved to fix one event, and **the other event started an hour late**. And workers sometimes drop out **only thirty minutes** before leaving.
>
> **So the real question isn't "is someone free?". It's "can we deliver this job without breaking something else?"**
> That's why we built an **AI Team Leader**. Let me show you, live.

Ready answers:
- **Why not a WhatsApp group?** "The group doesn't know Dritoni can't get from Vushtrri in time. That cost Magic Events an hour."
- **What if the AI is wrong?** "It can't assign anyone without the code checking the rules, can't approve by itself, and nothing is booked without the worker's accept."
- **Why didn't it force Erza?** "It never forces a person. When there's no safe option, the leader decides."
- **What if a worker says 'done' when they're not?** "Today progress is reported by the worker, like a check-in. Next step: order and timing checks that flag anything odd to the leader."
- **Why not Connecteam?** "A strong toolbox, but the manager still does the coordinating. We do it from one message and catch the conflict first."
- **Do you replace the PM?** "No. We take the coordination work; the client, the judgment and the approval stay with people."
- **What's simulated?** "The company and names are invented. The workflow, the agent and the phones are real and live."
- **Don't know?** "We haven't measured that yet. It's part of the pilot."
