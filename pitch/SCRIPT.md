## 3. TRINGA — how it works, why us, next step (3:15–4:45) · Slide 3

> What you just saw is one simple idea: **AI plans. Code checks. A human approves.**
>
> The AI reads messy messages and writes the tasks. Deterministic code checks every rule: skills, travel, working hours, vans and equipment. And people stay in control: the leader approves, the worker accepts, and nobody can force a rule break.
>
> We measured it in our run: **a full plan in 16 seconds, everyone confirmed 8 seconds after approval, and about 3 cents of AI per plan.**
>
> We're honest about the market: Connecteam, Microsoft Planner, Asana and Jira already help teams, some with AI. What we bring is **the whole handoff in one flow**, from the client's yes to the team doing the work, in the chat they already use, in Albanian.
>
> **Today: small field teams.** Next, the same agent becomes **a PM's assistant in larger companies**, with reports for each role, from the product manager to the developer.
>
> Our next step is concrete: a **proposed four-Saturday pilot with Magic Events**, measuring coordination time and mistakes.

Ready answers (from our notes):
- **Why not a WhatsApp group?** "The group doesn't know Dritoni can't get from Vushtrri in time. That cost Magic Events an hour."
- **What if the AI is wrong?** "It can't assign anyone without the code checking the rules, can't approve by itself, and nothing is booked without the worker's accept."
- **Why not Connecteam?** "It's a strong toolbox, but the manager still does the coordinating. We do it from one message and catch the conflict first."
- **Do you replace the PM?** "No. We take the coordination work. The client, the judgment and the approval stay with people."
- **Cost / who pays?** "About 3 cents per plan, measured; buttons cost nothing. For small businesses we include it in a monthly subscription."
- **What's simulated?** "The company and names are invented. The workflow, the agent and the phones are real and live."
- **Don't know?** "We haven't measured that yet. It's part of the pilot."
